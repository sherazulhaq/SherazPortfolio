About Me

Turning Numbers Into Strategic Decisions

I am Sheraz ul Haq, a Chartered Accountant (ICAP) and Director at S. Amer Associates, Chartered Accountants. With over two decades of experience, I have built a career spanning listed public companies, multinational groups, and leading advisory firms across Pakistan and the UAE.

My expertise covers financial planning & analysis, corporate finance, budgeting, investment appraisal, audit, and taxation. I have a proven track record of optimising financial performance, leading high-performing teams, and supporting strategic decision-making at the highest organisational levels.

Most notably, I served for over 16 years at Kohinoor Energy Limited — a public listed power project of Toyota Tsusho Corporation, Japan — where I led all aspects of financial management, compliance, and reporting.

ACA — ICAP PIPFA Fellow MIPA — Australia AFA — UK IFRS Specialist Tax & VAT Corporate Finance LUMS — Project Mgmt
